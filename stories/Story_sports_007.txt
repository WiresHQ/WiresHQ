Jordan Henderson, 36, scored his first two Chelsea goals in a 5-1 win over Bournemouth. Watch how the veteran midfielder rolled back time at Stamford Bridge.

Chelsea needed a response after a 3-0 defeat at Brentford before the international break, and they got one almost immediately at Stamford Bridge on Saturday 10 October 2026. Henderson, a former Liverpool captain and England midfielder who joined Chelsea on a free transfer in the summer, curled a finish in from Cole Palmer's cut-back barely a minute into the game. Morgan Rogers then made it 2-0 with a shot that deflected off Antonio Silva, giving Chelsea their earliest ever 2-0 lead in the Premier League.

The second half brought more goals. João Pedro made it 3-0 in the 46th minute, and Evanilson pulled one back for Bournemouth after a scramble from a set piece. Chelsea did not slip. João Pedro scored again in the 67th minute, and in the 70th minute Henderson completed his brace with a curled free kick from about 25 yards after Palmer was fouled. According to AFP, Henderson also set up Rogers' goal earlier in the match.

After the final whistle, Xabi Alonso praised the two beauties and said Chelsea signed Henderson for everything he brings, not just goals. The Chelsea head coach also admitted he had no explanation for the lightning start. The result lifts Chelsea to fifth in the Premier League table with 10 points and is their first league win in four matches. Bournemouth, under head coach Marco Rose, are still winless with 3 points. Sky Sports noted some nerves after Bournemouth scored, given Chelsea's defensive record, and AFP reports that Chelsea have now gone 22 league matches without a clean sheet. On the same day, Palmer signed a new contract that runs to 2034.

Chelsea are away at Everton on Saturday 17 October at 12:30 pm BST, which is 7:30 am ET, while Bournemouth host Sunderland on Sunday 18 October. Can Henderson keep his place, and can Chelsea fix their defence?
