Miami Vice 85 first look is here: Michael B. Jordan and Austin Butler are Tubbs and Crockett as cameras roll. See what Universal just revealed.

Universal Pictures released the first official images of the Miami Vice reboot on Monday 12 October 2026 and confirmed that Miami Vice '85 is now in production. The images show Michael B. Jordan as Detective Ricardo "Rico" Tubbs, bearded and dressed in all black with a leather jacket, and Austin Butler as Detective James "Sonny" Crockett, aiming a gun in a grey suit instead of the white suit many fans associate with the original series.

The movie is directed by Joseph Kosinski, the filmmaker behind Top Gun: Maverick and F1, from a screenplay by Dan Gilroy. It is filmed for IMAX and inspired by the pilot episode and first season of the 1980s NBC series, exploring the glamour and corruption of mid-1980s Miami. Unlike Michael Mann's 2006 film with Colin Farrell and Jamie Foxx, this version is a period piece set in 1985.

The supporting cast includes Alden Ehrenreich, Whitney Peak, Camila Morrone, Danny Ramirez, John Leguizamo, Andrew Schulz, Edi Gathegi, Walton Goggins, Andre Holland and Michael Irby. Filming has started, and reports on exact filming locations have not been officially confirmed.

Universal has set Miami Vice '85 for theaters on 19 May 2028 (May 19, 2028 in the US), after moving it from 6 August 2027. The UK release date has not been announced yet, and there is no trailer date so far.
