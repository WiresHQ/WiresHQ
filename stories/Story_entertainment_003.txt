RJ Mitte, the Breaking Bad actor who played Walter White Jr., has died aged 34. Here is what we know and how the cast is paying tribute to him.

RJ Mitte, born Roy Frank Mitte III, played Walter White Jr. in all five seasons of the AMC drama Breaking Bad from 2008 to 2013, appearing in 56 episodes. Like his character, he had cerebral palsy, and he became one of the most recognisable advocates for disability representation in film and television.

His representatives announced the death on Monday 12 October 2026, calling him a beloved actor, philanthropist and disability rights advocate and asking for privacy for his family and loved ones. A Justice of the Peace in Cameron County, Texas, a local official whose role in death inquiries is similar to a coroner, said he was pronounced dead in Brownsville in the early afternoon. His representative later confirmed that he died by suicide. No further details about the circumstances have been shared, and it would be wrong to speculate.

Bryan Cranston, who played his on-screen father Walter White, said he was in deep despair, called Mitte his sweet son and praised his advocacy for performers with disabilities. He said they had been texting about meeting for lunch only weeks earlier, and he ended his message with words of hope. Aaron Paul said he was heartbroken and told his friend he loved him, and Dean Norris said he had always looked out for Mitte as a young actor on set.

Away from Breaking Bad, Mitte was an ambassador for United Cerebral Palsy and Shriners Children's and president of The Mitte Foundation. He was part of the Breaking Bad ensemble that won a Screen Actors Guild Award. UK viewers may also remember him from Channel 4 coverage of the 2016 Rio Paralympics and appearances on The Last Leg.
