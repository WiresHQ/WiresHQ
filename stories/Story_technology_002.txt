iPhone Duo pre-orders open this Friday, 16 October, from £1,999 or $1,999. Here is the exact time, the release date and why stock could be tight.

Apple's first foldable iPhone was announced on 9 September at Apple Park, in the first major keynote led by John Ternus since he became Apple CEO on 1 September 2026. Now the iPhone Duo pre-order date is only days away, and buyers are searching for the price, the exact pre-order time and whether supply will run out.

The iPhone Duo pre-order time is Friday 16 October at 5:00 a.m. PT, which is 8:00 a.m. ET in the US and 1:00 p.m. UK time. Apple opened its get ready step on Monday 12 October, so you can pick your colour, storage, carrier and payment method before checkout opens. The iPhone Duo release date is Friday 23 October in more than 70 countries and regions, including the US and the UK, with 28 more countries following on Friday 30 October.

On price, the iPhone Duo starts at £1,999 for 256GB in the UK, including VAT, and from $1,999 in the US, before sales tax, so the final US checkout total will depend on your state. UK buyers can also see the 256GB model listed at £66.63 a month, while the US monthly option is $83.29 a month for 24 months. The phone is eSIM-only in both markets, so there is no physical SIM tray and you should check that your network supports eSIM.

Apple says the foldable iPhone has a 7.6-inch inner display and a 5.4-inch outer display, with the A20 Pro chip inside and iOS 27.1 on board. Those figures are Apple's own claims. Early hands-on impressions from several outlets say the crease is hard to see but still there, and no full independent review had been published at the time of writing. Credible supply reporting says Apple has built enough stock to launch this month but expects supply to stay limited, and many reviewers compare the phone with the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8.

What is still unknown is how fast pre-orders will sell out, what the delivery dates will be, and how the crease and hinge hold up over months of use.
