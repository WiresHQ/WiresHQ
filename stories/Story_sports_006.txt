Kerkez Ukraine list: Liverpool's Milos Kerkez was named on a Ukrainian website after a Hungary scuffle. Here is what we know, and what is still unclear.

The story began on 25 September 2026 at the Puskas Arena in Budapest, where Hungary lost 1-0 to Ukraine in the UEFA Nations League, the competition UEFA runs for national teams. Late in the football (soccer) match, Hungary captain Dominik Szoboszlai clashed with Ukraine winger Viktor Tsyhankov, and Kerkez ran in and grabbed Tsyhankov by the shirt collar. Both Kerkez and Szoboszlai play for Liverpool in the Premier League and for Hungary at international level.

Reports say Kerkez's father, Sebastijan, then posted a photo of the scuffle on his Instagram story, set to a song by Russian artists that was widely reported as Russian Paradise. Myrotvorets, a Ukrainian website that publishes personal details of people it considers hostile to Ukraine, then listed Kerkez and his father, reportedly as provocateurs accused of supporting Russian aggression. It matters that this is an independent website and not the Ukrainian government, and its accusations are not established facts.

The reaction came quickly. Hungary's foreign minister Anita Orban publicly condemned the listing and the possible threats against Kerkez and Hungarian politician Balazs Hidveghi, who is also named on the site. Kerkez told the Hungarian outlet Telex that he has never supported aggression and was only defending his captain and friend. We could not find a public statement from Liverpool. On the pitch, Kerkez scored the winner in Hungary's 1-0 win over Georgia on 2 October but picked up a second yellow card of the competition, and he missed the 2-1 win over Ukraine in Trnava, Slovakia on 5 October through suspension.

What happens next is still open. The Premier League returns this weekend, and Liverpool host Manchester City at Anfield on Sunday 11 October at 16:30 BST, which is 11:30 a.m. Eastern Time. Whether Kerkez plays has not been confirmed. It is also unclear whether Myrotvorets will remove the entries, whether Liverpool or the Hungarian football federation will comment, or whether UEFA will take any action over the scuffle, as nothing has been reported so far.
