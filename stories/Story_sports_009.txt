Lisandro Martinez has called out Manchester United's bosses after a 2-0 loss at Atletico Madrid, and the fallout is already shaking Michael Carrick's future.

Manchester United lost 2-0 at the Metropolitano in Madrid on Tuesday 13 October 2026 in the Champions League. Pablo Barrios scored after just 47 seconds, Leny Yoro was sent off in the 75th minute and Ademola Lookman added a second goal in the 79th. United created four big chances and failed to score with any of them, their most in a Champions League game without scoring since September 2012.

Straight after the final whistle, Lisandro Martinez told Amazon Prime Video that he expected more from technical director Jason Wilcox and from the people upstairs. He pointed to a summer in which United did not strengthen at left-back or striker, and he said it is too easy to blame the head coach and the players. When he was asked about Michael Carrick's future, the defender looked upwards, said he could not say more and pointed to other areas of the club.

The outburst came with United on their worst league start in 40 years and winless in five games in all competitions. Carrick said he had not seen the interview, put it down to emotion after the match and asked for the focus to move to Sunday's Premier League game at Leeds. Wayne Rooney said on Amazon Prime Video that Martinez had gone too far and that it should have stayed internal, but he also said the club does need to invest more. Jamie Carragher called the timing awful and said it will dominate the next few days.

There has been no official Manchester United statement on the interview so far. Talk of Carrick being sacked or Martinez leaving is speculation, not confirmed, and it is still unclear whether the club will act or back its head coach in public. Leeds United v Manchester United kicks off at 2:00pm BST on Sunday 18 October, with the Champions League game away at Como to follow.
