Pope Leo cancer news: the Vatican confirms Pope Leo XIV has lung adenocarcinoma after surgery in Rome. Watch what we know about his recovery so far.

The Vatican has confirmed that Pope Leo XIV has lung cancer. In a medical bulletin released on the evening of Tuesday, October 13, the Holy See Press Office said tests confirmed a diagnosis of lung adenocarcinoma, which pre-operative tests had suspected. Adenocarcinoma is the most common type of lung cancer. The diagnosis came after the 71-year-old pontiff had the lower lobe of his right lung removed at Gemelli hospital in Rome.

The story began on Sunday, October 11, when the Pope spoke to crowds in St Peter's Square after the Angelus, the Sunday noon prayer. He said doctors had found a small, localized nodule in his lung at a very early stage and that he would have it removed. Two days later he walked into Gemelli hospital, the Rome hospital popes have traditionally used, where a thoracic surgery team led by Dr Stefano Margaritora carried out the operation, also called surgery in the United States. The Vatican said about three hours were spent in the operating room, with the surgery itself lasting about 90 minutes.

According to the Vatican, the surgery was successful with no complications. Officials described the removal of the lobe as radical, which they said indicates that doctors removed all suspect tissue. The stage of the cancer has not been disclosed, and the Vatican said doctors are not currently speaking of chemotherapy or other therapies while further test results are pending. Early on Wednesday, October 14, the Vatican press office said the Pope had a peaceful night and promised more information later in the day.

Many people want to know about the Pope Leo recovery and when he might return to work. The Vatican expects a hospital stay of about one week, and all audiences are cancelled through October 20. No return-to-work date has been announced. His planned 11-day trip to Uruguay, Argentina and Peru, set for November 6 to 17, has not yet been confirmed or cancelled. Elected in May 2025, Pope Leo XIV is the first pope born in the United States and the head of a Church of about 1.4 billion members.
