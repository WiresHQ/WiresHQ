Lionel Messi's Argentina career comes to an end with a farewell friendly against Benin at the Estadio Más Monumental in Buenos Aires. The match was billed as "The Last Tango" and kicked off at 8:00 pm Argentina time on Tuesday 6 October (7 pm ET, midnight in the UK).

Messi, 39, leaves as Argentina's record appearance-maker and top scorer, with 125 goals in 207 games. He announced his international retirement in late August, about six weeks after Argentina lost the World Cup final to Spain 1-0 in New Jersey.

The Argentine Football Association then invited him back for one last home appearance. Around 85,000 fans were expected at the Monumental, with members of the 2022 World Cup-winning squad and their families invited to join the tribute.

Vice-captain Nicolás Otamendi, 38, is also being farewelled, and Rodrigo De Paul and Giovani Lo Celso were called up only for this game, so it may be their last appearance too. Coach Lionel Scaloni has called the night unforgettable and emotional.

Messi won the 2021 Copa América, the 2022 Finalissima, the 2022 World Cup and the 2024 Copa América with Argentina. He now returns to Inter Miami in MLS, and one question still hangs over the team: who inherits the No. 10 shirt.
