Man City vs PSG ended 2-4 after PSG led 3-0 inside 32 minutes at the Etihad. See the 99-second double and the Haaland fightback that shaped the night.

Paris Saint-Germain, the reigning European champions, took control of this Champions League league phase tie in Manchester. Désiré Doué opened the scoring in the 13th minute with a strike from distance, and 99 seconds later he set up Ousmane Dembélé, who headed in the second. Vitinha curled in the third in the 32nd minute, and PSG led 3-0 at half-time.

The match carried extra weight for Manchester City. It was their first home game since an independent commission found the club in breach of Premier League financial rules, a verdict City have appealed, and Pep Guardiola was in the stands. Head coach Enzo Maresca watched his side struggle badly in the first half in front of a record European home crowd of 60,021.

After the break it was a different City. Erling Haaland scored in the 53rd and 75th minutes to become the fastest player to reach 60 Champions League goals, getting there in 60 games, 20 quicker than Lionel Messi. Maghnes Akliouche had scored PSG's fourth in the 62nd minute, and that was the difference. Man City vs PSG finished 2-4, even though City had nearly twice as many shots as the visitors in the match.

For viewers new to the format, the Champions League league phase is a single table of 36 teams. PSG now have six points from two games, and Manchester City host AEK Athens on Tuesday 20 October at 8:00pm BST, 3:00pm ET. Whether you call it football or soccer, this was one of the wildest nights of the season so far.
