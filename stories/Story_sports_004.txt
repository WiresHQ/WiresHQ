Lionel Messi's final game for Argentina ended in a 3-0 win over Benin, with a penalty and two assists, as 85,000 fans said goodbye in Buenos Aires.

Messi played his 208th and last match for the Argentina national team on Tuesday, October 6, at the Monumental stadium in Buenos Aires. The friendly, billed by the Argentine Football Association as The Last Tango, was a sell-out, and the referee stopped play ten minutes after kickoff so the crowd and both teams could applaud the captain. Messi wore a special edition Albiceleste shirt with a golden number 10, and he was visibly emotional during the national anthem, with his three sons beside him.

The football (soccer) itself gave the farewell a fitting script. Messi took the corner that Nicolas Otamendi headed in to open the scoring in the 48th minute, then set up young playmaker Nico Paz for the second goal in the 61st. Ten minutes after that, he finished the night himself, sending a left-footed penalty into the top corner. Argentina won 3-0, a result that took second place to the occasion.

After the final whistle, Messi spoke to the crowd from the center of the pitch. He said that wearing the shirt for more than twenty years was a source of great pride, thanked his teammates for the titles they won together, and promised to keep supporting the national team from the fans' side. A drone show and fireworks closed the night, and Otamendi, who scored, also played his farewell match.

The farewell came after Messi announced his international retirement on August 31, about six weeks after Argentina lost the World Cup final to Spain 1-0. His Argentina career spans 21 years, 126 goals, the 2022 World Cup title and two Copa America wins, and he leaves as the national team's record scorer.
