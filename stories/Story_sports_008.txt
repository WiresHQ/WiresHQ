Coventry 4-1 Newcastle ended a 25-year wait for a Premier League home win. See how Lampard's Sky Blues stunned Newcastle on a historic Monday night.

Coventry vs Newcastle was the big story of Matchweek 6 in the Premier League. Frank Lampard's newly promoted side, a club that came up from the second tier, beat Newcastle United 4-1 at the Coventry Building Society Arena on Monday night. It was Coventry's first Premier League home win in 25 years and their second league win in a row. For American soccer fans, the stadium is often called the CBS Arena, and it has nothing to do with the CBS television network.

The Coventry City goals came quickly. Newcastle goalkeeper Lukáš Horníček fouled teenage striker Sidiki Cherif, and Jack Rudoni scored the penalty in the sixth minute, the first Premier League goal at the Coventry Building Society Arena. Three minutes into the second half, Cherif, making his first full Premier League start, doubled the lead. Sven Botman pulled one back with a long-range strike in the 70th minute, but substitutes Ellis Simms in the 81st minute and Stephen Mfuni in the 86th minute sealed a famous result.

The win lifted Coventry out of the relegation zone, meaning the bottom three places in the table that drop to the second division, and it also gave Lampard's Coventry their first home win in the top flight. Lampard, the former Chelsea and England midfielder, called it very satisfying but told his players to keep their feet on the ground before the trip to Tottenham next Monday.

For Newcastle, the night raised fresh questions about the defence under head coach Matthias Jaissle. Newcastle have conceded eight goals in their last two Monday night away league games, a 4-1 defeat at Leeds United and now this 4-1 defeat at Coventry. Jaissle said conceding that many in two away games is not good enough for the Premier League, and he apologised to the travelling supporters.
