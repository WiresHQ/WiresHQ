Apple HomeView is official: a £299 / $299 smart display with Siri AI. Here is the price, the release date and how it takes on the Echo Show.

HomeView is Apple's first smart display for the home. It pairs a 7-inch Liquid Retina screen with a speaker base, recognises up to six people by face and runs Siri AI, Apple's rebuilt and more conversational assistant. It comes as a tabletop model and a separate wall-mounted model, and the Home Camera view can show up to nine camera feeds at once. There is no App Store, so HomeView sticks to Apple's own apps and smart home controls.

The Apple HomeView price is £299 in the UK and $299 in the US. The UK price is reported at £299 including VAT, so check Apple's UK store before you buy, while the US price does not include sales tax. Pre-orders are open now in the UK, US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and India, and the Apple HomeView release date is 13 November. The wall-mounted model is also $299 but needs a separate mount, which pushes the total higher. HomeView is not on sale in the EU because Siri AI is not available there yet.

Apple also announced a new Apple TV 4K with an A19 chip and built-in microphones, priced at £299 / $299. That is a big jump from the $129 launch price of the 2022 model. HomePod mini 2 has an S10 chip, costs $149 in the US and goes on sale on 30 October, with Siri AI coming to it in a later free software update. UK prices for HomePod mini 2 are reported differently, so check Apple's UK store for the final figure.

Apple Home Collection is the whole-home part of the story. It is a range of cameras, a doorbell, a lock and thermostats made with LG Electronics and Schneider Electric, starting in December with select items in early 2027. In the UK, only the LG doorbell and cameras are listed so far, while the lock, thermostats and sensors are listed for the US and Canada. There are no independent reviews yet, only hands-on impressions, so how fast and accurate Siri AI is in real homes is still unproven.
