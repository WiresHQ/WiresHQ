WiresHQ is an independent online news publication. We cover the stories people are searching for and watching right now, then explain them properly: what happened, what is confirmed, and what is still unknown.

What we cover

  • Sports: football, cricket, live scores and tournaments.
  • Entertainment: films, TV series, streaming and new releases.
  • Celebrity: stars, musicians, politicians and public figures.
  • Tech & AI: new phones, AI tools and gadgets.
  • Economy & Finance: inflation, markets, gold, crypto, tariffs and exchange rates.

Why WiresHQ exists

Many people first meet a story in a short video. A video has thirty seconds. A story page has room for the source, the numbers, the date and the context. Every WiresHQ story is the full version of what you may have seen on video.

How we work

We build stories from official statements, original documents, data releases and reporting by established news organizations, and we name our sources in the text. We separate confirmed facts from claims, and we say when something is not yet confirmed. Our standards are set out in full in the editorial policy.
