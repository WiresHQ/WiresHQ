Who we are

WiresHQ is published by {{publisher}}. You can reach us at {{email}}.

Information we collect

We collect very little. There are three sources:

  • Server logs. When your browser requests a page, our hosting provider receives technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. This is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Described below.

WiresHQ has no user accounts, comments or newsletter, so we do not keep a database of readers.

Hosting

WiresHQ is served through Cloudflare, which delivers pages from servers around the world and protects the site from attacks. To do this, Cloudflare processes request data, including IP addresses. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

WiresHQ itself sets no tracking cookies. If you choose the light or dark theme with the toggle in the header, your browser stores one item in local storage, whq-theme, to remember the choice. It stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Our advertising partner and embedded videos can set their own cookies, described below. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site works without them.

Advertising

WiresHQ shows ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to WiresHQ or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to WiresHQ and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also visit optout.aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ad cookies and similar technologies are used. You can change your choice at any time using the controls in the consent message.

Ads are labeled “Advertisement” and kept apart from our stories. Advertisers do not influence what we publish.

Embedded video and sharing

Some stories embed YouTube videos using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode. Loading an embedded video connects your browser to YouTube, and YouTube may collect information about you when you play it. See the Google privacy policy. The share buttons on stories are ordinary links: they contact WhatsApp, Facebook or X only if you click them.

How we use information

We use the information above to deliver and secure WiresHQ, to find and fix technical problems, to answer your messages and to show ads. We do not sell the messages you send us, and we do not use them for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep emails for as long as we need to answer them and to keep a record of corrections, then delete them. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider under its own retention schedule.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to or restrict how it is used. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

WiresHQ is a general-audience news site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
