What to write to us about

  • News tips. Send what you know, with any links or documents, and tell us how we can verify it. Email a tip.
  • Corrections. Point us to the sentence and, if you can, to a source. See how we handle corrections. Report an error.
  • Advertising and partnerships. Business enquiries.
  • Privacy. Questions about our privacy policy or requests about your data. Email us.
  • Anything else. We read every message.

When to expect a reply

We aim to reply within three working days. Corrections about a living person are looked at first.
