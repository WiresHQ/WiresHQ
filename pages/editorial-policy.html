WiresHQ exists to give readers the full story behind what is trending. That only works if the story is accurate, so these are the rules we hold ourselves to.

Our standards

  • Accurate. We lead with what is confirmed and give names, numbers, units and dates.
  • Attributed. We say where information comes from.
  • Clear. Plain words and short sentences. No headline promises more than the story delivers.
  • Fair. We treat people with respect, especially in tragedies and obituaries, and we do not publish rumors about private lives.

Sourcing

We build stories from official statements, original documents, data releases and reporting by established news organizations. We name the source in the text or link to it. When a claim comes from a single source, or is not confirmed, we say so. We tell you what is known and what is not.

Review before publishing

An editor checks each story against its sources before it goes live. For finance and sports stories, we also re-check figures, units and dates.

Videos and stories

Each story page is the full version of the video it accompanies. If we find an error in a video, we correct the story page and mark the correction there.

Use of AI tools

We may use AI tools to help with research, translation and drafting. A human editor reviews and takes responsibility for every story, and nothing is published automatically. We do not use AI to invent quotes, sources or images of real events.

Images

We use images that we own, have licensed or have permission to use, and we credit them where required. We do not use an image in a way that misleads about what it shows. If you own an image on WiresHQ and want it credited or removed, email us.

Corrections and updates

When we get a fact wrong, we fix it and tell you:

  • Correction. A significant factual error is fixed and marked with a dated “Correction” note at the top of the story.
  • Update. New information is added and the story shows an “Updated” time.
  • Typos. Spelling and formatting errors are fixed without a note.

We do not delete a story to hide a mistake. We may remove or change a story for legal or safety reasons, or to protect someone’s privacy.

Report an error

Email {{email}} with the subject “Correction” and the link to the story. Point us to the sentence and, if you can, to a source. We aim to review every report within three working days, and sooner when an error concerns a living person.

Advertising and independence

Ads are labeled “Advertisement” and kept apart from our stories. Advertisers cannot see or change a story before it is published, and they do not influence what we cover. WiresHQ does not publish sponsored articles at this time; if we ever do, they will be clearly labeled.
